HOW TO EDIT THE PATIENT EMAIL
=============================

THE EMAIL TEXT
  Open "Email Template.docx" in Word, edit it, and save. The next time you
  click a patient's email button, the app uses the new text - no restart
  needed.

  - The first line, "Subject: ...", is the email's subject line.
  - {greeting} becomes "Dear John Doe," (or "Dear Mr. Doe," / "Dear Ms. Doe,"
    when the rows you copied from Excel include a SEX or GENDER column).
  - You can also use {first} and {last} for the patient's first/last name.
  - Bold, italics, underline, and links carry over into the email.
    Fonts, colors, text sizes, tables, and pictures do not - the email
    uses Outlook's normal font.
  - Keep the file's name and location exactly as they are.
  - Your signature is not part of the template. Outlook adds it.

THE ATTACHMENTS
  Every file in the "Attachments" folder is attached to the email.
  To change what gets sent, add, remove, or replace files in that folder.
  An empty folder means no attachments.
